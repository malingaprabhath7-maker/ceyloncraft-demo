Where it is made

Each craft has a home. These are the places our pieces come from.

01

Ambalangoda

A coastal town known for generations of mask carvers who make masks for Kolam dance and Sanni rituals from light Kaduru wood.

02

Kandy

The old royal city, home of Kandyan brass work and batik studios that still draw by hand with hot wax.

03

Molagoda

A pottery village on the Kandy road where families shape and fire red clay pots, lamps and cookware.

04

Hapuvida

A village near Matale famous for Laksha work: wood turned on a lathe and coloured with heated lacquer.

05

Dumbara

The Dumbara valley near Kandy, where weavers make strong hemp mats with old geometric and animal motifs.

06

Galle

Beeralu lace has been made around Galle since the Portuguese period, by twisting cotton threads on wooden bobbins.
